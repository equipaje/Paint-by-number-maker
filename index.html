Quick fixes

Your drawing is used exactly as loaded. Turn on a fix only if it helps, and each one makes a small change.

Darkness (for grayish images) 128

Touch up by hand

Drag to draw a line and lift to paint it. To snap it straight across or straight up and down, keep a second finger on the screen while you drag (or hold Shift).

Brush size 2 mm

Paint black or white to fix spots in the drawing. Erase removes your touch-ups there. Touch-ups stay in place when you change the quick fixes.

Save the drawing

The black-and-white drawing on its own, at print size, without cells or numbers.

Cells

The drawing changed since the cells were made. Making cells again replaces your splits and merges, but your areas stay.

Edit cells

Tap cells to select them. Drag to move around, and pinch or use the buttons to zoom.

Merge joins the selected cells. With a single cell selected, it merges into the neighbor it shares the most edge with.

Areas

Give part of the picture, like the sky, the floor or a crowd, its own cut pattern. Pick an area, then tap a black shape to add all of it, or brush over cells. The brush always adds whole cells.

Changing the pattern of "Whole picture" redoes the cells everywhere that isn't in another area, including your edits there.
